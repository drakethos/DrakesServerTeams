## Team Ward

An obsidian-dark guard stone that glows in its team's color, with the team's icon and name above it. Always on; team members have access, others are kept out of building and chests (doors are a switch). Admins press **Shift+E** on it for its settings.

![Two Team Wards, day](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/drakethos/DrakesServerTeams/main/docs/images/ward-day.png)
![Two Team Wards, night](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/drakethos/DrakesServerTeams/main/docs/images/ward-night.png)

![Shift+E settings (admins)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/drakethos/DrakesServerTeams/main/docs/images/ward-settings.png)

## Spawn stones

A team can have its own copy of the start stones at its spawn point (opt-in). The original start location stays the true spawn for everyone else.

![A team's own start stones, day](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/drakethos/DrakesServerTeams/main/docs/images/spawn-stones-day.png)
![At night](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/drakethos/DrakesServerTeams/main/docs/images/spawn-stones-night.png)

## Admin tools

The admin panel (`teams panel` or F6) and the Teams tab in DrakesTestLab's toolbox (F9) put players on teams, set spawn points, edit colors and icons, and manage the nearest Team Ward.

![The admin panel](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/drakethos/DrakesServerTeams/main/docs/images/admin-panel.png)
![The DrakesTestLab Teams tab](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/drakethos/DrakesServerTeams/main/docs/images/lab-tab.png)
